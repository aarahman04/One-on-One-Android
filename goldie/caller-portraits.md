# Fictional caller portraits

Generated using the built-in imagegen tool, with no reference images. These are fictional adults used as still video frames by `StoreCallActivity`; the call UI and controls come from the real app.

- Arjun: `app/src/debug/res/drawable-nodpi/store_arjun.png`
- Maya: `app/src/debug/res/drawable-nodpi/store_maya.png`

## Arjun prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: fictional male video caller portrait for an Android app store screenshot. Create a natural smartphone front-camera portrait of Arjun, a clearly adult Indian man around 28 years old, short dark hair, neat light beard, warm friendly smile, looking directly at the camera while seated in a cozy home at dusk. Portrait 2:3 composition, head and upper torso centered; his full hair, face and shoulders fit with generous room above the head and at the sides so this can crop to a narrow phone screen. His face should be in the upper-middle of the frame; leave the lower third as his plain dark teal shirt and softly blurred room, for the app's real call controls to overlay. Soft warm window light, realistic skin texture, candid video chat feel, tasteful muted colors. Only the portrait photograph: no phone, no UI, no text, no logos, no watermark. This is an entirely fictional adult, not a real person's likeness.
